About VJD App Studio

An independent Android application development brand specializing in Android Studio, Java and XML.

Who we are

VJD App Studio is an independent Android application development brand. We design and build Android apps that aim to be useful, easy to understand and pleasant to use.

This website is the home of our apps. Here you can explore what we have built, read the details of each app and download it from Google Play or as an APK file when a download is available.

How we build

Our apps are developed with the standard native Android toolset:

  • Android Studio
  • Java
  • XML layouts

We focus on clear screens, simple flows and practical features rather than complexity for its own sake.

What we care about

  • Usefulness: each app should solve a real, everyday need.
  • Simplicity: clean, user-friendly design that is quick to learn.
  • Transparency: downloads happen only when you click, and we show a safety notice for APK files.
  • Respect for your device: we recommend Google Play when an app is listed there.
